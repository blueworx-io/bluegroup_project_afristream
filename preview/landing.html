Setup guides

Pick your device, follow the steps

The real steps for the devices people actually use. Work through them yourself, or book a call and we will do it with you.

Fire TV Stick

Amazon’s stick, into any TV with a spare HDMI port. About fifteen minutes from box to first play.

  1. Plug the stick into a spare HDMI port and its power lead into the mains — the TV’s own USB port rarely gives it enough power.
  2. Switch the TV to that HDMI input, then pair the remote when it asks.
  3. Join your Wi-Fi and sign in with an Amazon account. A free one is enough; you only need Prime if you subscribe to Prime Video.
  4. Open the Appstore and install the apps for the services you actually pay for.
  5. Sign in to each app with your own account details for that service.
  6. Sign in to AfriStream on your phone or laptop, and use it to look up what to watch.

We can do all of this with you on a call, or set the stick up before it reaches you.

Apple TV

Apple’s box. The setup does most of the work for you if you already have an iPhone.

  1. Plug the box into HDMI and the mains, and switch the TV to that input.
  2. Hold an unlocked iPhone near it to copy your Wi-Fi and Apple Account across, or type both in by hand.
  3. Finish the on-screen setup and let it install any waiting update.
  4. Install the apps for the services you subscribe to from the App Store, and sign in to each with your own details.
  5. Sign in to AfriStream on your phone or laptop to search.

Apple keeps some app sign-ins behind the Apple Account rather than the app itself, which catches people out. Worth doing the first one with us.

Android / Google TV

A Chromecast with Google TV, an Android box, or a set with Google TV built in.

  1. Plug it in, switch the TV to that HDMI input and pair the remote.
  2. Join Wi-Fi and sign in with a Google account, or finish setup from the Google Home app on your phone.
  3. Install your subscription apps from the Play Store and sign in to each one.
  4. Reorder the apps row so the ones you use sit first — recommendations are above your own apps by default.
  5. Sign in to AfriStream on your phone or laptop to search.

Cheap unbranded Android boxes often ship with old firmware and apps that will never update. We will tell you if yours is one of them.

Samsung TV

Samsung sets run Tizen, with their own store built in. No extra device needed.

  1. Finish the on-screen setup and connect the set to your Wi-Fi.
  2. Sign in with a Samsung account — several apps will not install without one.
  3. Open the Apps tile and install the services you subscribe to.
  4. Sign in to each app. Where it shows a pairing code, type that on your phone rather than fighting the remote.
  5. Run a software update from Settings, then restart the set.
  6. Sign in to AfriStream on your phone or laptop to search.

Sets older than roughly 2017 stop getting new versions of some apps. We will check yours before you spend anything.

LG TV

LG sets run webOS, with the LG Content Store built in.

  1. Finish setup, connect to Wi-Fi and accept the terms — the store stays locked until you do.
  2. Create or sign in to an LG account.
  3. Open the LG Content Store and install the apps for your subscriptions.
  4. Sign in to each with your own account.
  5. Check for a firmware update in Settings, then restart the set.
  6. Sign in to AfriStream on your phone or laptop to search.

Getting comfortable with the Magic Remote pointer trips people up more than any of the sign-ins. We will walk you through it.

Laptop

Nothing to install. Every service and AfriStream itself run in a browser.

  1. Use an up-to-date Chrome, Edge, Firefox or Safari — older browsers get blocked or dropped to a low resolution.
  2. Sign in to each service’s own website with your own account.
  3. To watch on the TV, run an HDMI cable across or cast the browser tab where the service allows it.
  4. Sign in to AfriStream in the same browser and search from there.

Some services cap browser playback below the quality their app gives you. That is their limit, not your setup.

Phone or tablet

Useful on its own, and the easiest way to type sign-in codes for everything else.

  1. Install the apps for the services you subscribe to from the App Store or Play Store.
  2. Sign in to each with your own account.
  3. Set downloads to Wi-Fi only if your data is metered.
  4. Cast or AirPlay to the TV where the app allows it.
  5. Sign in to AfriStream in your phone’s browser to search.

A few apps block casting for licensing reasons. Where that happens, installing the app on the TV itself is the way round it.
